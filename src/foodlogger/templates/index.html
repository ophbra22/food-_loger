BEHIND THE JOURNAL

Small app. Real machine learning.

FoodLogger is a food-recognition and nutrition journal built with Python, FastAPI, TensorFlow and SQLite.

Recognition, with room for uncertainty

The default model is MobileNetV2 trained on ImageNet, with 23 food classes linked to this catalog. It suggests one main food at a time. Scores are model outputs, not measured accuracy. Other foods, mixed dishes and recipes may be misidentified.

Your portion makes the difference

A photo cannot tell us the weight. You select the food and enter grams; the app scales an illustrative per-100g nutrition entry. These illustrative estimates vary with ingredients and preparation and should not guide dietary decisions.

Your account and photos

Your journal is stored on our server and is accessible through your account. Photos are processed in memory on the server and are not saved. Barcode numbers are sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information; your photos and account details are not shared with that service. Save your recovery code: it is the only way to reset a forgotten password.

Product data

Product information comes from Open Food Facts contributors under the Open Database License. Coverage and completeness vary. Review the label, especially serving size and grams versus millilitres.
