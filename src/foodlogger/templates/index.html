BEHIND THE JOURNAL

Small app. Real machine learning.

FoodLogger is a Python portfolio project combining FastAPI, TensorFlow and SQLite.

Recognition, with room for uncertainty

The default model is MobileNetV2 trained on ImageNet, with 23 food classes linked to this catalog. It suggests one main food at a time. Scores are model outputs, not measured accuracy. Other foods, mixed dishes and recipes may be misidentified.

Your portion makes the difference

A photo cannot tell us the weight. You select the food and enter grams; the app scales an illustrative per-100g nutrition entry. These estimates are for demonstrating the project, not dietary decisions.

Local by design

Images are processed in memory and not saved. The journal lives in a local SQLite file. The first prediction downloads public model weights; photos are not sent to an external service.
